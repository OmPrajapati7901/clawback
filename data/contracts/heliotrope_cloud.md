# Master Services Agreement — Heliotrope Cloud Hosting, Inc.

*Managed cloud hosting services for Harborline Industries, Inc.*

This Agreement is entered into by Heliotrope Cloud Hosting, Inc. ("Provider") and Harborline Industries, Inc. ("Customer") as of February 1, 2025. It sets out the services, the rates, the volume bands and the included services that apply to every invoice Provider issues during the Term.

## 1.1 Parties and Roles

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made by and between Heliotrope Cloud Hosting, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Provider"), and Harborline Industries, Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business in Cleveland, Ohio ("Customer"). Provider and Customer are each a "Party" and together the "Parties". Provider shall perform the services described in Section 2.1 through its own employees and subcontractors, and remains responsible for their performance.

## 1.2 Effective Date and Term

This Agreement takes effect on February 1, 2025 (the "Effective Date") and continues for a Term of three (3) years from that date (the "Term") unless terminated earlier under Section 9.1. On expiration of the initial Term the Agreement renews for successive twelve (12) month periods unless a Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current period. All amounts are stated in United States dollars.

## 2.1 Services Provided

Provider shall provide managed cloud hosting, compute instances, object storage, managed PostgreSQL and bandwidth services delivered from the region Customer selects in the service console. The only charges that may be invoiced under this Agreement are the rates and bands expressly identified in Section 3.2 and Section 4.1, and the pass-through amounts permitted by Section 3.3. No other charges, surcharges, administrative fees, minimum commitments or accessorials may be billed unless Customer authorizes them in writing before they are incurred.

## 2.2 Service Standards and Remedies

Provider shall make compute and managed database services available at least ninety-nine and ninety-five hundredths percent (99.95%) of the time in each calendar month. Availability shortfalls earn service credits as set out in Exhibit C, and those credits are the sole remedy for an availability shortfall. A service failure gives rise to the credits stated in this Section and does not create a right to increase any rate, to suspend service without notice, or to add a recovery surcharge of any kind.

## 3.1 Invoicing and Payment

Provider shall invoice monthly in arrears. Each invoice must state a unique invoice number, the invoice date, the applicable purchase order number, and for every line item the SKU code, the description, the quantity, the unit price and the line amount. The quantity multiplied by the unit price must equal the line amount, and the invoice total must equal the sum of the line amounts. Customer pays undisputed amounts within 30 days of receipt of a correct invoice. An invoice that does not comply with this Section is returned for correction without affecting the Term.

## 3.2 Rates and Charges

The rates below are the complete schedule of charges for the services described in Section 2.1, stated per unit of measure. A standard compute instance (SKU HC-100) is charged at $0.42 per instance-hour. A managed PostgreSQL instance (SKU HC-300) is charged at $310.00 per instance-month. Bandwidth overage beyond the committed allowance (SKU HC-400) is charged at $0.09 per GB of overage. Snapshot storage retention (SKU HC-500) is charged at $0.05 per GB-month. Rates are exclusive of sales and use taxes, which Customer pays as required by law. No fuel, minimum, platform, administrative or convenience charge may be added to these rates.

## 3.3 Pass-Through Charges

Customer-approved third-party costs are passed through at actual cost with no markup and must be identified on the invoice by the SKU to which they relate. A pass-through amount that is not pre-approved in writing is not payable. Provider bears all costs of doing business under this Agreement, including its own insurance, licensing, equipment and supervision.

## 4.1 Volume Bands and Quantity Pricing

Charges for object storage (SKU HC-200) are calculated incrementally rather than on the whole quantity. The first 49,999 GB-months billed on a line are charged at $0.02 per GB-month, and the reduced rate of $0.015 per GB-month applies only to units in excess of the first 49,999 GB-months on that line. A further reduction to $0.01 per GB-month applies to units in excess of the first 249,999 GB-months on the same line. A line may not be split, combined or re-billed to move units between bands, and quantities on separate lines are not aggregated for the purpose of reaching a band.

## 5.1 Included Services

Standard support during business hours (SKU HC-900) and monthly usage and billing reporting (SKU HC-901) are included in the rates in Section 3.2 and in the storage charges in Section 4.1. Neither may be billed as a separate line item, and neither may be added as a percentage surcharge on platform charges.

## 5.2 Charges Not Permitted

Provider shall not bill separately for travel time, training, supervision, administrative overhead or any service described in Section 5.1. A charge for a service identified in Section 5.1 may not appear on an invoice with a positive amount, and a credit for such a charge may only be applied with Customer's written agreement.

## 6.1 Purchase Orders and Reporting

Each invoice must reference a Customer purchase order with the HL-HC- prefix, and Provider shall not invoice against an expired or closed purchase order. Provider shall also provide a quarterly business review covering volumes, service performance and any substitute products, substitutions or rate changes applied during the quarter.

## 7.1 Adjustment Mechanism

Provider may change platform architecture, instance families or storage media at any time provided that the rates in Section 3.2 and the bands in Section 4.1 are not increased. Service credits under Section 2.2 and taxes are the only amounts that may appear on an invoice in addition to the charges calculated from Section 3.2 and Section 4.1.

## 7.2 Price Adjustments

The rates in Section 3.2 and the storage bands in Section 4.1 are firm and fixed for the entire Term. Provider shall not increase any rate, remove any band discount or introduce a platform fee, minimum commitment or index adjustment during the Term. A unit price on an invoice that exceeds the rate stated in Section 3.2 is incorrect and is not payable as billed.

## 8.1 Audit and Records

Customer or its designee may audit Provider's records supporting any invoice for 24 months after the invoice date. If an audit shows an overcharge, Provider shall refund or credit the overcharged amount within thirty (30) days of the audit findings, together with interest at one percent (1%) per month from the invoice date. Provider shall keep supporting records for the full audit period.

## 8.2 Disputed Amounts

Customer may withhold a disputed amount and shall give written notice within 30 days of receiving the invoice. The Parties shall resolve the dispute in good faith within thirty (30) days of notice, and Provider shall not suspend service or impose a late fee on a disputed amount while the dispute is pending. Amounts found to be overcharges are credited on the next invoice.

## 9.1 Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement for material breach on thirty (30) days' written notice if the breach is not cured within that period, or for convenience on 90 days' written notice. Termination does not affect Customer's right to recover overcharges identified under Section 8.1, and the rates in Section 3.2 continue to govern all services performed before the effective date of termination.

## 10.1 Governing Law and Entire Agreement

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. This Agreement, together with its Exhibits, is the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior proposals, quotes and rate sheets. No purchase order, invoice, portal term or click-through term modifies this Agreement, and any such term that conflicts with this Agreement is void.
