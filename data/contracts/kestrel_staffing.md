# Master Services Agreement — Kestrel Staffing Partners

*Contract staffing services for Harborline Industries, Inc.*

This Agreement is entered into by Kestrel Staffing Partners ("Staffing Provider") and Harborline Industries, Inc. ("Customer") as of August 1, 2023. It sets out the services, the rates, the volume bands and the included services that apply to every invoice Staffing Provider issues during the Term.

## 1.1 Parties and Roles

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made by and between Kestrel Staffing Partners, a Texas limited liability company ("Staffing Provider"), and Harborline Industries, Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business in Cleveland, Ohio ("Customer"). Staffing Provider and Customer are each a "Party" and together the "Parties". Staffing Provider shall perform the services described in Section 2.1 through its own employees and subcontractors, and remains responsible for their performance.

## 1.2 Effective Date and Term

This Agreement takes effect on August 1, 2023 (the "Effective Date") and continues for a Term of three (3) years from that date (the "Term") unless terminated earlier under Section 9.1. On expiration of the initial Term the Agreement renews for successive twelve (12) month periods unless a Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the then-current period. All amounts are stated in United States dollars.

## 2.1 Services Provided

Staffing Provider shall provide contract staffing services for the analyst, engineering and administrative roles catalogued in Exhibit B, including recruiting, screening and assignment administration. The only charges that may be invoiced under this Agreement are the rates and bands expressly identified in Section 3.2 and Section 4.1, and the pass-through amounts permitted by Section 3.3. No other charges, surcharges, administrative fees, minimum commitments or accessorials may be billed unless Customer authorizes them in writing before they are incurred.

## 2.2 Service Standards and Remedies

Staffing Provider shall replace an assigned worker who does not meet the assignment requirements within three (3) business days at no additional charge, and shall reconcile approved timesheets weekly rather than at invoice time. A service failure gives rise to the credits stated in this Section and does not create a right to increase any rate, to suspend service without notice, or to add a recovery surcharge of any kind.

## 3.1 Invoicing and Payment

Staffing Provider shall invoice monthly in arrears. Each invoice must state a unique invoice number, the invoice date, the applicable purchase order number, and for every line item the SKU code, the description, the quantity, the unit price and the line amount. The quantity multiplied by the unit price must equal the line amount, and the invoice total must equal the sum of the line amounts. Customer pays undisputed amounts within 30 days of receipt of a correct invoice. An invoice that does not comply with this Section is returned for correction without affecting the Term.

## 3.2 Rates and Charges

The rates below are the complete schedule of charges for the services described in Section 2.1, stated per unit of measure. A contract analyst (SKU KS-100) is invoiced at $78.00 per hour worked. A senior engineer (SKU KS-200) is invoiced at $145.00 per hour worked. A data entry clerk (SKU KS-300) is invoiced at $42.00 per hour worked. A permanent placement fee (SKU KS-400) is invoiced at $2,500.00 per accepted placement. Rates are exclusive of sales and use taxes, which Customer pays as required by law. No fuel, minimum, platform, administrative or convenience charge may be added to these rates.

## 3.3 Pass-Through Charges

Customer-approved third-party costs are passed through at actual cost with no markup and must be identified on the invoice by the SKU to which they relate. A pass-through amount that is not pre-approved in writing is not payable. Staffing Provider bears all costs of doing business under this Agreement, including its own insurance, licensing, equipment and supervision.

## 4.1 No Volume Bands

This Agreement contains no volume rate bands or tiered discounts. Hours are billed at the hourly rates in Section 3.2 regardless of the number of hours invoiced in a month, and no blended rate, overtime multiplier or volume discount applies.

## 5.1 Included Services

Timesheet administration and approval (SKU KS-900) and the monthly utilization and compliance report (SKU KS-901) are included in the hourly rates in Section 3.2. Neither may be billed separately, and the placement fee covers all recruiting, screening, onboarding and background-check administration.

## 5.2 Charges Not Permitted

Staffing Provider shall not bill separately for travel time, training, supervision, administrative overhead or any service described in Section 5.1. A charge for a service identified in Section 5.1 may not appear on an invoice with a positive amount, and a credit for such a charge may only be applied with Customer's written agreement.

## 6.1 Purchase Orders and Reporting

Each invoice must reference a Customer purchase order with the HL-KS- prefix, and Staffing Provider shall not invoice against an expired or closed purchase order. Staffing Provider shall also provide a quarterly business review covering volumes, service performance and any substitute products, substitutions or rate changes applied during the quarter.

## 7.1 Adjustment Mechanism

Rates are stated per hour worked. Staffing Provider shall not bill for orientation, training, equipment, benefits administration or travel time, and shall not invoice hours beyond the timesheets Customer approved for the period invoiced.

## 7.2 Price Adjustments

Staffing Provider may adjust the hourly rates in Section 3.2 on each anniversary of the Effective Date by no more than four percent (4.0%) of the rate in effect immediately before that anniversary, compounding annually over the Term. An adjustment may not be applied before the anniversary on which it takes effect and may not be applied to hours worked in an earlier period. The placement fee in Section 3.2 is fixed for the Term.

## 8.1 Audit and Records

Customer or its designee may audit Staffing Provider's records supporting any invoice for 24 months after the invoice date. If an audit shows an overcharge, Staffing Provider shall refund or credit the overcharged amount within thirty (30) days of the audit findings, together with interest at one percent (1%) per month from the invoice date. Staffing Provider shall keep supporting records for the full audit period.

## 8.2 Disputed Amounts

Customer may withhold a disputed amount and shall give written notice within 20 days of receiving the invoice. The Parties shall resolve the dispute in good faith within thirty (30) days of notice, and Staffing Provider shall not suspend service or impose a late fee on a disputed amount while the dispute is pending. Amounts found to be overcharges are credited on the next invoice.

## 9.1 Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement for material breach on thirty (30) days' written notice if the breach is not cured within that period, or for convenience on 60 days' written notice. Termination does not affect Customer's right to recover overcharges identified under Section 8.1, and the rates in Section 3.2 continue to govern all services performed before the effective date of termination.

## 10.1 Governing Law and Entire Agreement

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. This Agreement, together with its Exhibits, is the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior proposals, quotes and rate sheets. No purchase order, invoice, portal term or click-through term modifies this Agreement, and any such term that conflicts with this Agreement is void.
