# Master Services Agreement — Pinecrest Office Supply Company

*Office supply, breakroom and furniture products for Harborline Industries, Inc.*

This Agreement is entered into by Pinecrest Office Supply Company ("Supplier") and Harborline Industries, Inc. ("Customer") as of September 1, 2024. It sets out the services, the rates, the volume bands and the included services that apply to every invoice Supplier issues during the Term.

## 1.1 Parties and Roles

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made by and between Pinecrest Office Supply Company, an Oregon corporation ("Supplier"), and Harborline Industries, Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business in Cleveland, Ohio ("Customer"). Supplier and Customer are each a "Party" and together the "Parties". Supplier shall perform the services described in Section 2.1 through its own employees and subcontractors, and remains responsible for their performance.

## 1.2 Effective Date and Term

This Agreement takes effect on September 1, 2024 (the "Effective Date") and continues for a Term of three (3) years from that date (the "Term") unless terminated earlier under Section 9.1. On expiration of the initial Term the Agreement renews for successive twelve (12) month periods unless a Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the end of the then-current period. All amounts are stated in United States dollars.

## 2.1 Services Provided

Supplier shall provide office supplies, breakroom products, paper and furniture delivered to the Customer ordering locations listed in Exhibit A. The only charges that may be invoiced under this Agreement are the rates and bands expressly identified in Section 3.2 and Section 4.1, and the pass-through amounts permitted by Section 3.3. No other charges, surcharges, administrative fees, minimum commitments or accessorials may be billed unless Customer authorizes them in writing before they are incurred.

## 2.2 Service Standards and Remedies

Supplier shall deliver stocked items by the next business day for orders released before 3:00 p.m. local time and shall maintain a fill rate of at least ninety-eight percent (98%) measured monthly. Backordered lines are reported to Customer within one business day. A service failure gives rise to the credits stated in this Section and does not create a right to increase any rate, to suspend service without notice, or to add a recovery surcharge of any kind.

## 3.1 Invoicing and Payment

Supplier shall invoice monthly in arrears. Each invoice must state a unique invoice number, the invoice date, the applicable purchase order number, and for every line item the SKU code, the description, the quantity, the unit price and the line amount. The quantity multiplied by the unit price must equal the line amount, and the invoice total must equal the sum of the line amounts. Customer pays undisputed amounts within 30 days of receipt of a correct invoice. An invoice that does not comply with this Section is returned for correction without affecting the Term.

## 3.2 Rates and Charges

The rates below are the complete schedule of charges for the services described in Section 2.1, stated per unit of measure. A high-yield black toner cartridge (SKU PS-200) is priced at $92.50 per cartridge. A breakroom supply box (SKU PS-300) is priced at $46.75 per box. An ergonomic task chair (SKU PS-400) is priced at $215.00 per chair. A two-drawer filing cabinet (SKU PS-500) is priced at $168.00 per cabinet. Rates are exclusive of sales and use taxes, which Customer pays as required by law. No fuel, minimum, platform, administrative or convenience charge may be added to these rates.

## 3.3 Pass-Through Charges

Customer-approved third-party costs are passed through at actual cost with no markup and must be identified on the invoice by the SKU to which they relate. A pass-through amount that is not pre-approved in writing is not payable. Supplier bears all costs of doing business under this Agreement, including its own insurance, licensing, equipment and supervision.

## 4.1 Volume Bands and Quantity Pricing

Copy paper (SKU PS-100) is priced at $38.40 per case before volume discounts and is billed on a whole-line basis. When the quantity billed on a single invoice line reaches fifty (50) cases, the rate of $34.25 per case applies to every case on that line, and when the quantity billed on that line reaches two hundred (200) cases, the rate of $29.90 per case applies to every case on that line. Discounts that apply only to cases above a threshold are not offered under this Agreement, and the whole-line rate is determined by the quantity actually invoiced on the line.

## 5.1 Included Services

Standard delivery and inside placement (SKU PS-900) and the monthly spend and usage report (SKU PS-901) are included in the rates in Section 3.2 and in the case rates in Section 4.1. No delivery, handling, fuel, small-order or reporting fee may be added to an invoice.

## 5.2 Charges Not Permitted

Supplier shall not bill separately for travel time, training, supervision, administrative overhead or any service described in Section 5.1. A charge for a service identified in Section 5.1 may not appear on an invoice with a positive amount, and a credit for such a charge may only be applied with Customer's written agreement.

## 6.1 Purchase Orders and Reporting

Each invoice must reference a Customer purchase order with the HL-PS- prefix, and Supplier shall not invoice against an expired or closed purchase order. Supplier shall also provide a quarterly business review covering volumes, service performance and any substitute products, substitutions or rate changes applied during the quarter.

## 7.1 Adjustment Mechanism

Supplier may substitute an equivalent product for a discontinued item without changing the SKU or the rate, provided the substitute is of equal or better specification and the substitution is disclosed on the next monthly report.

## 7.2 Price Adjustments

The rates in Section 3.2 and the case rates in Section 4.1 are firm and fixed for the entire Term. Supplier shall not increase any price during the Term, and an invoice line that states a price above the applicable rate is incorrect, is not payable as billed and may be deducted by Customer from the amount payable. Delivery surcharges, small-order fees and index adjustments are not permitted.

## 8.1 Audit and Records

Customer or its designee may audit Supplier's records supporting any invoice for 12 months after the invoice date. If an audit shows an overcharge, Supplier shall refund or credit the overcharged amount within thirty (30) days of the audit findings, together with interest at one percent (1%) per month from the invoice date. Supplier shall keep supporting records for the full audit period.

## 8.2 Disputed Amounts

Customer may withhold a disputed amount and shall give written notice within 15 days of receiving the invoice. The Parties shall resolve the dispute in good faith within thirty (30) days of notice, and Supplier shall not suspend service or impose a late fee on a disputed amount while the dispute is pending. Amounts found to be overcharges are credited on the next invoice.

## 9.1 Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement for material breach on thirty (30) days' written notice if the breach is not cured within that period, or for convenience on 30 days' written notice. Termination does not affect Customer's right to recover overcharges identified under Section 8.1, and the rates in Section 3.2 continue to govern all services performed before the effective date of termination.

## 10.1 Governing Law and Entire Agreement

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. This Agreement, together with its Exhibits, is the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior proposals, quotes and rate sheets. No purchase order, invoice, portal term or click-through term modifies this Agreement, and any such term that conflicts with this Agreement is void.
