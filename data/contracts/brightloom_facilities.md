# Master Services Agreement — Brightloom Facilities Services

*Facilities management services for Harborline Industries, Inc.*

This Agreement is entered into by Brightloom Facilities Services ("Contractor") and Harborline Industries, Inc. ("Customer") as of January 15, 2024. It sets out the services, the rates, the volume bands and the included services that apply to every invoice Contractor issues during the Term.

## 1.1 Parties and Roles

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made by and between Brightloom Facilities Services, an Illinois corporation ("Contractor"), and Harborline Industries, Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business in Cleveland, Ohio ("Customer"). Contractor and Customer are each a "Party" and together the "Parties". Contractor shall perform the services described in Section 2.1 through its own employees and subcontractors, and remains responsible for their performance.

## 1.2 Effective Date and Term

This Agreement takes effect on January 15, 2024 (the "Effective Date") and continues for a Term of three (3) years from that date (the "Term") unless terminated earlier under Section 9.1. On expiration of the initial Term the Agreement renews for successive twelve (12) month periods unless a Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the then-current period. All amounts are stated in United States dollars.

## 2.1 Services Provided

Contractor shall provide janitorial, hard-floor care, landscaping and HVAC preventive maintenance services at the Customer facilities listed in Exhibit A. The only charges that may be invoiced under this Agreement are the rates and bands expressly identified in Section 3.2 and Section 4.1, and the pass-through amounts permitted by Section 3.3. No other charges, surcharges, administrative fees, minimum commitments or accessorials may be billed unless Customer authorizes them in writing before they are incurred.

## 2.2 Service Standards and Remedies

Contractor shall complete a quarterly quality inspection at each site and shall score at least eighty-five percent (85%) on the inspection checklist. A site that scores below that threshold is re-performed within five (5) business days at no additional charge. A service failure gives rise to the credits stated in this Section and does not create a right to increase any rate, to suspend service without notice, or to add a recovery surcharge of any kind.

## 3.1 Invoicing and Payment

Contractor shall invoice monthly in arrears. Each invoice must state a unique invoice number, the invoice date, the applicable purchase order number, and for every line item the SKU code, the description, the quantity, the unit price and the line amount. The quantity multiplied by the unit price must equal the line amount, and the invoice total must equal the sum of the line amounts. Customer pays undisputed amounts within 45 days of receipt of a correct invoice. An invoice that does not comply with this Section is returned for correction without affecting the Term.

## 3.2 Rates and Charges

The rates below are the complete schedule of charges for the services described in Section 2.1, stated per unit of measure. A standard janitorial service visit (SKU BF-100) carries a rate of $185.00 per visit. HVAC preventive maintenance on a rooftop unit (SKU BF-200) carries a rate of $420.00 per rooftop unit serviced. A landscaping service visit (SKU BF-300) carries a rate of $140.00 per visit. An emergency maintenance callout (SKU BF-400) carries a rate of $250.00 per callout. Rates are exclusive of sales and use taxes, which Customer pays as required by law. No fuel, minimum, platform, administrative or convenience charge may be added to these rates.

## 3.3 Pass-Through Charges

Customer-approved third-party costs are passed through at actual cost with no markup and must be identified on the invoice by the SKU to which they relate. A pass-through amount that is not pre-approved in writing is not payable. Contractor bears all costs of doing business under this Agreement, including its own insurance, licensing, equipment and supervision.

## 4.1 Volume Bands and Quantity Pricing

Floor care (SKU BF-500) is billed by measured area in three bands. The first 19,999 square feet billed on a line carry a rate of $0.14 per square foot; every square foot from the 20,000th through the 74,999th on that line carries a rate of $0.11 per square foot; and every square foot from the 75,000th onward on that line carries a rate of $0.085 per square foot. Each square foot is billed in exactly one band, and the lower band rates do not apply to the whole quantity.

## 5.1 Included Services

Restroom consumables and paper products (SKU BF-900) are included in the janitorial rate for every site, and the monthly service report and inspection log (SKU BF-901) is included in the preventive maintenance and landscaping rates. Neither SKU may be billed separately, and a consumables charge may not be added as a percentage of the janitorial rate.

## 5.2 Charges Not Permitted

Contractor shall not bill separately for travel time, training, supervision, administrative overhead or any service described in Section 5.1. A charge for a service identified in Section 5.1 may not appear on an invoice with a positive amount, and a credit for such a charge may only be applied with Customer's written agreement.

## 6.1 Purchase Orders and Reporting

Each invoice must reference a Customer purchase order with the HL-BF- prefix, and Contractor shall not invoice against an expired or closed purchase order. Contractor shall also provide a quarterly business review covering volumes, service performance and any substitute products, substitutions or rate changes applied during the quarter.

## 7.1 Adjustment Mechanism

Rates are stated per visit, per rooftop unit, per callout or per square foot as indicated in Section 3.2 and Section 4.1. An emergency callout outside business hours is billed at the rate in Section 3.2 with no travel-time, fuel or administrative additive.

## 7.2 Price Adjustments

Contractor may adjust the rates in Section 3.2 once per contract year by written notice given at least thirty (30) days before the anniversary of the Effective Date, subject to a ceiling of three percent (3.0%) of the rate in effect immediately before that anniversary, compounded annually over the Term. The floor-care band rates in Section 4.1 are not subject to any annual adjustment and remain fixed for the Term. A rate may not be raised more than once in any twelve (12) month period and may not be applied retroactively.

## 8.1 Audit and Records

Customer or its designee may audit Contractor's records supporting any invoice for 12 months after the invoice date. If an audit shows an overcharge, Contractor shall refund or credit the overcharged amount within thirty (30) days of the audit findings, together with interest at one percent (1%) per month from the invoice date. Contractor shall keep supporting records for the full audit period.

## 8.2 Disputed Amounts

Customer may withhold a disputed amount and shall give written notice within 20 days of receiving the invoice. The Parties shall resolve the dispute in good faith within thirty (30) days of notice, and Contractor shall not suspend service or impose a late fee on a disputed amount while the dispute is pending. Amounts found to be overcharges are credited on the next invoice.

## 9.1 Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement for material breach on thirty (30) days' written notice if the breach is not cured within that period, or for convenience on 60 days' written notice. Termination does not affect Customer's right to recover overcharges identified under Section 8.1, and the rates in Section 3.2 continue to govern all services performed before the effective date of termination.

## 10.1 Governing Law and Entire Agreement

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. This Agreement, together with its Exhibits, is the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior proposals, quotes and rate sheets. No purchase order, invoice, portal term or click-through term modifies this Agreement, and any such term that conflicts with this Agreement is void.
